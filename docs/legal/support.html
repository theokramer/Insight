Get help with Insight

Email theo.kramer.bus@gmail.com with your device model, app version, and a description of the problem. Keep credentials and private study images out of your message.

Common questions

GPT cannot connect? Check your internet connection and active Insight Pro subscription. Your imported images remain saved, and you can edit masks manually.

Missing a purchase? Restore from the subscription screen using the store account that made the purchase.

Cancel a subscription? Use your Apple or Google account's subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel billing.

Delete your pictures? Open a diagram's editor and delete it, or delete its collection. There is no cloud library sync in this version.
