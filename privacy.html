Privacy Policy

Updated October 14, 2026

Insight is provided by Theo Kramer. This policy explains how the native Insight app and its image-analysis service handle your information.

Your library

Your imported images, PDF page images, masks, collections, and study history are saved on your device. Insight does not provide a cloud library account or library synchronization. Your operating system may include app data in device backups under its own settings. Deleting a diagram or collection removes its local library records and managed images. Uninstalling removes the local library; it does not cancel a subscription.

Optional image analysis

Before your first GPT analysis, Insight asks for permission to share images with OpenAI through the Insight service hosted on Cloudflare. Only images you choose to analyze, their detected text and text positions, any optional learning focus you enter, and the requested card-detail setting are sent. OpenAI returns proposed labels and explanations. Editing masks and studying do not upload your images. You can withdraw permission for future image analysis in Settings and continue using manual masks.

The service does not maintain a cloud image library or intentionally log image content, OCR text, or generated answers. Requests are processed to return results. We request store: false from OpenAI. This is not a promise of zero retention: OpenAI's standard API abuse-monitoring retention is generally up to 30 days, with applicable exceptions. OpenAI states API content is not used for model training by default. See its API data controls.

Purchases and service operation

Apple or Google handles store billing. RevenueCat processes store transaction information, purchase history, product identifiers, subscription status, and an app customer identifier to manage purchases and restores. The Insight backend receives that identifier to verify entitlement before paid image analysis. Superwall presents paywalls and processes paywall interaction events, subscription status, and device/app information. Insight does not receive your payment card details.

Cloudflare hosts the service and processes connection information such as IP addresses, request timing, and response status for delivery, security, and operation. Infrastructure logs, subscription records, and provider security records have the retention periods described by those providers and applicable legal requirements. Insight does not include advertising or request permission to track you across other companies' apps.

Provider information: Cloudflare, RevenueCat, Superwall, Apple, and Google.

Permissions and support

Photo, camera, and file access is used when you choose those import methods. Insight does not request precise location, contacts, or microphone access. If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever information you include. Do not include private images or credentials unless needed for your request. Support correspondence is kept while resolving your request and as necessary for follow-up or legal obligations.

Your choices and rights

You may use manual mask creation and studying without sending images to OpenAI. You can delete local content, withdraw future analysis permission, change system permissions, and manage or cancel subscriptions through your store account. Contact theo.kramer.bus@gmail.com for privacy questions or requests concerning service-held information. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to processing, or receive a portable copy of personal data, and to complain to your local data protection authority. We may need information to verify your request.

Where applicable, we process data to provide features and subscriptions you request, based on your consent for optional image sharing, and for legitimate interests in security and support. Providers may process information outside your country under their applicable safeguards. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already performed.

Children and updates

Insight is a general learning tool, not a child-directed service. Do not send children's personal information for image analysis without appropriate authorization. Contact us if you believe such information was shared without permission. Material changes to this policy will be reflected here and, where required, in the app before new processing takes place.
